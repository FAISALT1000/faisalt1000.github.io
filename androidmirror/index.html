AndroidMirror

Your phone and your Android TV, together

Control the TV from your phone, share your screen and photos, and keep everything between your own devices on your home Wi‑Fi. No account, no ads, no servers.

Get the app

AndroidMirror on Google Play

One app for both: install it on your phone and on your Android TV (search for AndroidMirror in the TV's Play Store). Then scan the QR code on the TV's home screen with your phone's camera to link them.
